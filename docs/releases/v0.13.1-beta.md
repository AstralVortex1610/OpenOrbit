**Orbit is in beta.** Expect rough edges, and please open an issue when something breaks.

## What's new
- **Phone Link (beta): use Orbit from your Android phone.** Turn it on in Settings → Phone Link, press *Pair a phone*, and scan the QR code with the new **Orbit Remote** app (`OrbitRemote-0.1.0.apk`).
  - Orbit still runs on your laptop. Your keys, files, browser and models stay there, and the phone never gets an API key.
  - Everything between the phone and the laptop is end-to-end encrypted inside Orbit, with keys from the QR pairing.
  - On the same Wi-Fi, the phone connects directly. Elsewhere, it uses a free Cloudflare quick tunnel (bundled; no account) or your own relay.
  - Chat, projects, tasks, memory, Orbiter and Settings work on the phone, plus file sending and downloads both ways. When the laptop is away, messages wait in a queue and send when it's back.
  - Off by default. Paired phones can be revoked at any time.
- **Never use third-party services.** One switch keeps Cloudflare and ntfy.sh out of Phone Link completely. Phones on other networks can then still connect through your own relay.
- **Self-hosted relay** in `relay/`: a ~100-line Node server, or a Worker on your own Cloudflare account. See `relay/README.md`.
- Full write-up of how it works and what each service can and cannot see: `docs/phone-link.md`.

## Fixes
- Phone Link pairing only ever runs one load at a time, and shows a readable laptop name.
- The Companion talk button no longer errors when it's missing from the page.

## Under the hood
- The Windows build bundles `cloudflared` 2026.11.1, checked against Cloudflare's published SHA256SUMS before it's packed.
- New tests: `link-proto-test` (protocol and crypto), `link-e2e` (full sessions over LAN, tunnel and relay, with a check that no plaintext reaches Cloudflare, ntfy or the relay), and `remote-ui-smoke` (the phone UI in Chromium).

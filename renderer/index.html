Live voice

A real back-and-forth call with Orbit. It hears your tone, reacts, and you can cut in mid-sentence. The voice model talks; Orbit's own agent does the work behind it.

Orbit hears you, thinks with your own model and speaks with a natural voice, all on this laptop. No voice key. The first call downloads about 200 MB once (Phonon voice + hearing); after that it works offline (except for the model you chose as its brain). Headphones keep it from hearing itself.
